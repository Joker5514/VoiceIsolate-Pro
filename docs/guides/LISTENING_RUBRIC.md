# VIP-004 box 5 — Blinded listening rubric

Pairs live in `output/corpus-eval/listen/` (generated by
`scripts/quality/corpus-eval.cjs --stems`). Each pair is one clip's
input vs one chain's output, in randomized A/B order. The mapping is
sealed in `listen/key.json` — **do not open it until every pair is
scored**, or the blinding is void.

## Procedure

1. Listen on headphones, in a quiet room, at a comfortable fixed volume.
   Do not adjust volume between A and B of a pair.
2. Listen to A, then B, then A again if unsure. Score each side
   separately first, then the preference.
3. Score all 32 pairs before opening `key.json`. Record scores in
   `output/corpus-eval/listen/scores.md` (create it; it is git-ignored,
   so it stays local to the listener).
4. After scoring, unseal the key and tabulate: for each chain, mean
   scores per category and the win rate (pairs where the chain's side
   was preferred over the input side).

## Dimensions (1–5 each, per side)

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speech clarity | Words unintelligible | Most words clear, some effort | Every word effortless |
| Naturalness | Robotic, muffled, or phasey | Mostly natural, slight processing audible | Indistinguishable from unprocessed speech |
| Noise remaining | Noise dominates | Noise audible but speech leads | No audible noise |
| Artifacts | Musical noise / warbling / dropouts throughout | Occasional artifact | None heard |
| Fricative / whisper preservation (quiet + whispered pairs) | Sibilants / breath gone or harsh | Present but dulled | Fully present, natural |

## Preference (per pair)

- **A clearly better / A slightly better / Tie / B slightly better /
  B clearly better**, plus one line on what decided it (e.g. "B removed
  the HVAC hum without thinning the voice").

## How many examples is enough

32 pairs (8 classes × 4 chains, one clip per class) is the prepared
minimum, not a large-sample study. Report means with the pair count and
do not over-claim: a chain wins a category only if it wins a majority
of that category's pairs *and* its objective class mean (SI-SDR delta
or preservation) agrees in direction. If listening and objective
metrics disagree, that disagreement is itself a finding to record, not
something to average away.

## What this does not prove

One clip per class cannot certify a chain for a whole class. Listening
is the perceptual check on top of the 36-clip objective eval, not a
replacement for it. Chains are judged for selection only when objective
bars (CORPUS_THRESHOLDS.md) and listening agree.
