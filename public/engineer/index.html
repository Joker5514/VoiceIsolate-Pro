The contract

Two kinds of controls.
Zero surprises.

Every control declares when it acts. Live controls move sound instantly. Process controls are captured as one immutable snapshot the moment you press Process — dragging one never re-runs the AI.

37

Live-Mix controls

Gains, EQ bands, dynamics, gate, and de-esser drive AudioParams on the already-separated stems. Immediate, smooth, reversible — the neural net is never involved.

27

Process-time spectral controls

Mask shaping, floors, and spectral surgery travel in a versioned snapshot to the inference worker. One explicit run per file and configuration.

Forensics

Inspect the evidence.

A spectrogram-first workspace with time-frequency selection: isolate a region, audition the Removed (Delta) view to hear exactly what the process took out, and verify before you commit.

  • Analysis overlays appear only from real analysis — never placeholders
  • Source confidence, voice/noise balance, and SNR from measured signal
  • Raw source stays immutable; processed and removed are derived views
Speakers

Operate on voices,
not just waveforms.

On-device speaker detection clusters the clean stem by voice timbre — mute, solo, or rebalance each speaker with instant gain automation. Enroll a target voiceprint to focus the process on one voice in a crowd.

  • Whisper-level speech still detected — low-energy voices aren't discarded
  • Per-speaker boost up to +6 dB for faint voices
  • All diarization and enrollment stays on the device
