What you get

Separation runs as an explicit batch process in an isolated worker — never from a slider, never in the cloud. When it finishes, you hold two independent, full-resolution stems.

Stem 01

Clean voice

Speech reconstructed through a masked inverse STFT — the voice, isolated from everything around it. Solo it for transcripts, boost it for podcasts, or carve it with EQ.

Stem 02

Background

The sample-wise residual — everything the voice mask removed: room tone, hum, traffic, crowd. Audit it with the residual monitor to hear exactly what was taken out.

How it works

One pass. No phase damage.

The fused spectral chain runs one forward STFT and one inverse STFT — masks for voice, noise, and speech multiply in a single representation, so the audio never passes through repeated analysis damage.

  • Batch ONNX inference in a worker — WebGPU when available, WASM fallback
  • Model integrity verified by SHA-256 before every session
  • Sliders and mix moves never re-trigger the AI
Live-Mix

Separated once.

Stems play through a zero-ML playback graph: per-stem gains, a full EQ rack, dynamics, a real-time gate and de-esser. Every move is immediate and reversible — the AI already did its part.
