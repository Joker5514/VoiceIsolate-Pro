2 · WhisperHunter AI

Best after Analyze Full Audio — collaborates with the workspace above to remove unwanted sound and lift voices/whispers.

Removes unwanted background sound. The gate silences the gaps between words, while spectral NR learns the steady noise (hiss, hum, fans) and subtracts it. Example: clean up a phone interview recorded next to an air conditioner — set NR Amount ~70% and a Gate Threshold around −40 dB.

Ten tone controls from deep bass to bright "air". Boost a band to bring it forward, cut it to push it back. Example: for a thin laptop-mic voice, add ~+2 dB Body and +1.5 dB Presence; for a boomy close mic, cut ~−4 dB Bass.

Evens out volume. The compressor brings loud and soft words closer together; the limiter sets a hard ceiling so nothing clips. Example: a guest who keeps drifting from the mic — Threshold ~−24 dB, Ratio 4:1, Makeup +3 dB for a steady, broadcast-level voice.

Surgical frequency tools: high-/low-pass filters to trim the extremes, a de-esser to tame harsh "s" sounds, tilt for quick brighter/darker, and formant shift for vocal character. Example: a sharp, sibilant narrator — De-ess Amount ~6 dB at 7 kHz, HP Freq 90 Hz to drop rumble.

Restoration and stereo tools: dereverb pulls a voice out of an echoey room, harmonic recovery rebuilds detail lost to noise reduction, and width/phase shape the stereo image. Example: speech recorded in an empty hall — Dereverb ~50% with Rev Decay raised toward 70% for the long tail.

Final mastering: overall volume trim, the dry/wet blend between original and processed, output stereo width, and export dither. Example: if cleanup sounds too aggressive, pull Dry/Wet to ~75% to mix a little of the natural original back in.

Isolates the voice from everything else. Focus on the speech frequency band and push down whatever sits outside it, plus cancel stereo bleed between two mics. Example: lift a speaker out of background music — Voice Iso ~85% with BG Suppress ~60%.

Extreme voice isolation for whispers buried under club noise, crowd roar, and music. Example: nightclub recording with a whisper at −55 dBFS — Whisper Mode Forensic, Crowd Null ~88%, Bass Crush ~95%.
