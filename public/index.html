Why it’s different

  • 100% local audio — ONNX runs in a Web Worker on your device (WebGPU when available). No cloud inference API for your files.
  • Privacy by architecture — no microphone, no telemetry, nothing leaves the device for isolation.
  • Live-Mix after one separation — rebalance voice vs background instantly without re-running ML.
  • Engineer Mode when you need depth — Creator · Studio · Forensic racks, same privacy model.
