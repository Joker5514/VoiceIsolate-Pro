Voice isolation for recorded audio

Hear What Others Miss. Clean voice. Keep the evidence.

Choose a recording and VoiceIsolate Pro splits it into a clean voice and the background, then lets you remix the two. For interviews, meetings, voice memos and field recordings where speech is hard to hear. Processing happens on this device, so your audio is never uploaded to our servers.

One forward STFT. In-place spectral work. One inverse STFT. Audio is decoded locally; batch ML never runs from a slider.

  1. 01

    Input

    Upload-only file pick. No microphone.

  2. 02

    Decode / resample

    Local decode to 48 kHz buffers.

  3. 03

    Stem separation

    Batch ONNX in a worker (BS-RNN default).

  4. 04

    Spectral processing

    Fused masks on one STFT representation.

  5. 05

    Reconstruction

    One inverse STFT. No extra phase passes.

  6. 06

    Live-Mix

    Zero-ML playback graph: gains, EQ, gate, de-esser.

  7. 07

    Export

    Local encode. Optional user-initiated Drive save.
